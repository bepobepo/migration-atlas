# Historical UK long-term immigration, emigration and net migration

The UK Parliament House of Commons Library’s *Migration statistics* briefing, published 27 May 2026, provides the underlying data workbook for its charts. Sheet 2.1(1) reproduces the UK Office for National Statistics long-term international migration series. This archive extracts the all-citizenship annual estimates for calendar years ending in December from 1991 through 2011, with thousands converted to persons. From 2012 the atlas uses its separate pinned ONS administrative series. [Briefing](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/sn06077/) · [Workbook](https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/SN06077/CBP06077-data.xlsx)

The older LTIM series adjusts passenger survey information for additional migration flows, including Irish migration and asylum and change-of-intention adjustments. The Parliament briefing describes a best available historical IPS-only series starting in 1964. Its survey coverage lacks the adjustments that support a whole-UK total. The export therefore begins in 1991. The charts’ estimates of annual average net migration in earlier decades are interval averages. The importer does not assign them to individual years.

Post-census revisions to net migration in 2001–2011 were published independently of gross counts. For 2001, 481,000 arrivals and 309,000 departures accompany published net migration of +179,000. The application must preserve the published net. Original ONS Table 2.01a archived 1 December 2016 matches all selected figures from 1991 to 2008. Some later figures were revised. The ONS release of 27 August 2020 also independently reproduces the compiled revised 2010 and 2011 observations in full. [ONS Table 2.01a and editions](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration/datasets/longterminternationalmigrationcitizenshiptable201a/current) · [ONS LTIM, August 2020](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/internationalmigration/datasets/migrationstatisticsquarterlyreportprovisionallongterminternationalmigrationltimestimates/august2020)

Run `python scripts/import_historical_ons.py` to rebuild the export and `python scripts/import_historical_ons.py --check` to verify all 63 published values from the pinned spreadsheet. The archive checksum and dates are stored in [source.json](source.json).
